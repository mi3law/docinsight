DocInsight

See how a Google Doc was written: a timeline of its revision history, the moments when large amounts of text arrived at once, and the writing-process indicators behind them. Nothing leaves your browser.

Open a record

A record exported from the DocInsight extension (.html or .json). No sign-in needed.

Check one Doc by its link

Sign in with your school Google account, then paste the link of a Doc you can edit. DocInsight reads its revision history with your own access, the same full history the extension reads.

About

For regular use, install the DocInsight extension: it adds a button to every Google Doc you open and needs no sign-in. See the install guide.

DocInsight shows evidence of the writing process. It does not judge whether text "is AI", and it sends nothing anywhere: no server of its own, no analytics, no copies kept. With sign-in, Google gives this page a token that can read your Drive files for about an hour; the page uses it only to read the history of the Doc you name, keeps it in this tab, and never stores or shows it.

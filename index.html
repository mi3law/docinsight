Help

Use it on every Doc

Get the Chrome extension: it adds a DocInsight button to every Google Doc you open. No sign-in, no links to paste.

Download the extension

  1. Unzip the download and keep the folder somewhere it will stay, such as Documents.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode, top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open any Google Doc. A blue DocInsight button appears beside Share.

DocInsight isn't in the Chrome Web Store, which is why Chrome loads it this way. It only runs on Google Docs, and tells you when a newer version is out.

Open a record

A record exported from DocInsight (.html or .json), such as one a colleague sent you. No sign-in needed.

Nothing leaves your browser: no server, no analytics, no copies kept.

About DocInsight

DocInsight shows evidence of the writing process: a timeline of a Doc's revision history, the moments when large amounts of text arrived at once, and the indicators behind them. A large insertion is a fact to see and discuss with the student; where it came from is for you to find out.

When you check a Doc by its link, Google gives this page a token that can read your Drive files for about an hour. The page uses it only to read the history of the Doc you name, keeps it in this tab, and never stores or shows it.
